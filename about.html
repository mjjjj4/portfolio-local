About me

Hi, I'm Madelyn.

I'm a multidisciplinary creator based in Irvine, specializing in photography, web design, UX/UI, architectural rendering, branding, and AI optimization.

I approach each project by first establishing a clear visual direction through mood boards, references, and cohesive design systems. From there, I focus on making sure every element works together visually while also serving a clear purpose.

My background in consumer psychology at UCI has influenced the way I think about design and branding. I'm interested in more than just how something looks—I care about how people respond to visuals, what captures their attention, and what ultimately makes them want to engage.

I've always been an artist at heart, which makes me especially attentive to the craft and details behind a project. Whether I'm creating a photograph, architectural render, website, or brand identity, I want the final result to feel intentional, cohesive, and thoughtfully executed.
